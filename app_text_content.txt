EDUCATIONAL INTRODUCTION
================================================================================

Section Title: What are Word Vectors?

Content:
In AI, words are represented as vectors (lists of numbers) in a high-dimensional space. Words with similar meanings have vectors that point in similar directions.

Cosine similarity measures how similar two word vectors are, ranging from -1 (opposite) to 1 (identical). Values close to 1 mean the words are semantically related!

================================================================================
VECTOR LEARNING MODULE - CAT/DOG EXAMPLE
================================================================================

Section Title: How Vector Similarity Works: Simple Example

Introduction:
Let's understand the math with a simple 3-dimensional example using "cat" and "dog":

--- Step 1: Words as Vectors ---
Each word is represented as a list of numbers (a vector). In this simple example:

cat = [0.12, -0.03, 0.44]
dog = [0.10, -0.01, 0.47]

--- Step 2: Multiply Matching Numbers (Dot Product) ---
Multiply each pair of corresponding values and add them all up:

Table:
Word    Value 1    Value 2    Value 3
cat     0.12       -0.03      0.44
dog     0.10       -0.01      0.47
Multiply ×  ↓          ↓          ↓
Result  0.012      0.0003     0.2068

Add them all: 0.012 + 0.0003 + 0.2068 = 0.2191
This is called the "dot product"

--- Step 3: Find the "Length" of Each Vector ---
Calculate the magnitude (length) of each vector using: √(value₁² + value₂² + value₃²)

cat length:
Square each value
0.12² + (-0.03)² + 0.44²
Add them together
0.0144 + 0.0009 + 0.1936 = 0.2089
Take the square root
√0.2089 ≈ 0.457

dog length:
Square each value
0.10² + (-0.01)² + 0.47²
Add them together
0.01 + 0.0001 + 0.2209 = 0.231
Take the square root
√0.231 ≈ 0.481

--- Step 4: Put It All Together! ---
Divide the dot product by the product of the two lengths:

Cosine Similarity Formula:
(dot product) ÷ (length₁ × length₂)

= 0.2191 ÷ (0.457 × 0.481)
= 0.2191 ÷ 0.2197
≈ 0.997

0.997 ≈ 99.7% similar!

HOW AI USES THIS SECTION
================================================================================

Section Title: How AI Uses This for Text Prediction

Content:
1. Context Understanding: When AI sees "The quick brown cat jumped over the...", it converts each word to vectors and understands the context.

2. Next Word Prediction: The AI looks for words with vectors similar to the context. Words like "fence" or "wall" would have higher similarity scores than unrelated words like "pizza".

3. Probability Calculation: Based on similarity scores and patterns learned from billions of examples, the AI assigns probabilities to possible next words and chooses the most likely one.

Key Insight: The closer two words are in vector space, the more semantically related they are. This is why similar words have high similarity scores while unrelated words don't.
